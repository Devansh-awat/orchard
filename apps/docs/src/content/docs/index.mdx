## What it does

Orchard is a self-hosted platform that sits on top of Kubernetes. You install it
into a cluster you control, and it turns that cluster into something closer to a
PaaS: connect a repo, get builds, an HTTPS URL, a Postgres database next to your
app, live logs, cron jobs, rollbacks, and a team model with roles and SSO.

Nothing phones home. There is no hosted control plane in the middle: the
scheduler is your cluster, the registry is yours, and the data stays on your
hardware.
