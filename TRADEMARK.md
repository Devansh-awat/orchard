# Trademark policy

Orchard's code is licensed under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). That license covers the code. It does not
grant any right to use the names and logos below, which this policy calls the marks. This policy is
about the marks only and takes away none of your rights under the AGPL to copy, modify, run and
share the code.

## The marks

- The **Orchard** name and logo, which identify this project. Orchard is a
  [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com) project.
- The **Hack Club** name and logo, and the Orpheus mascot, which belong to The Hack Foundation, doing
  business as Hack Club.

## What you can do without asking

- Run unmodified copies of Orchard, for yourself or for others, under the Orchard name.
- Say factually what your software or service works with, for example "compatible with Orchard",
  "runs on Orchard" or "a graft for Orchard".
- Say truthfully that your version is based on Orchard, for example "a fork of Orchard".
- Refer to Orchard or Hack Club in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews.

## What you need to do if you distribute a modified version

Forks must rebrand before redistributing a modified version. Give it a different name, and replace
the assets listed below with your own, so nobody mistakes your version for the original.

You don't have to rename code identifiers, packages or configuration, and you must keep the copyright
and license notices the AGPL requires. Using the code privately, or running a modified version for
your own organization without presenting it as Orchard, needs no rebrand.

## What needs permission

- Using a mark in the name of a product, service, company or domain.
- Using a logo in a way that suggests Hack Club or the Orchard project made, endorses or sponsors
  your work.
- Selling merchandise with a mark on it.

To ask, open an [issue](https://github.com/hackclub/orchard/issues) or email
[hi@leafd.dev](mailto:hi@leafd.dev).
